title: About Us
---

MKM Structural Steel is a Victorian steel fabrication business covering the full arc of structural steelwork — detailing, supply, fabrication and erection.

Because every stage stays under one roof, nothing gets lost between the drawing office and the site. The drawings we detail are the drawings our fabricators work from, and the steel our workshop produces is stood by our own erection crews.

We work with builders, engineers and owners across commercial, industrial and residential projects throughout Victoria — from single residential members to complete structural packages.
